Everything a small business needs

No subscriptions, no cloud requirement, no feature gates.

GST, done right

CGST/SGST/IGST split, HSN/SAC, GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B style summaries with one-click Excel export.

e-Way bills

Generate NIC e-way bills straight from the invoice — sandbox and production endpoints.

Inventory

Units, godowns, stock items, sales/purchase stock lines and stock summaries.

Multi-user teams

Optional Kite Team server: roles, audit log, per-company data, access from any browser or phone.

AI quick entry

Describe a voucher in one sentence — AI drafts it for review. Bring your own key; drafts never auto-post.

Offline & private

One SQLite file per company on your disk. Copy it anywhere to back up. You own your books.

PDF invoices

Tax-invoice preview, download and email via your own SMTP.

Double-entry core

Balanced books enforced before every save; day book, trial balance, P&L, balance sheet.
