Caveats, guardrails & security

Clear limits so you know what Kite does — and does not — protect.

Installers & trust
  • Binaries are unsigned — expect SmartScreen / Gatekeeper warnings.
  • Download only from official GitHub Releases.
  • No auto-update channel — you choose when to upgrade.
Data & backups
  • You own the SQLite files. Losing the only copy loses the books — back up deliberately.
  • Do not put live company DBs on a multi-writer network share for Solo (corruption risk).
  • For shared books, use Enterprise (HTTP to the parent), not file sharing.
Enterprise / LAN
  • Plain HTTP on a trusted LAN is convenient but session tokens are not encrypted — use HTTPS beyond the office LAN.
  • Open firewall port 8080 only as needed; keep jwt_secret.hex with your backups.
  • Passwords are PBKDF2-hashed. Roles limit UI actions; they are not a wall against someone who has the DB files.
AI (optional)
  • Off until you add a provider key. Draft-only — nothing auto-posts.
  • When enabled, prompts and book context go to your chosen provider (leaves the machine).
  • Always verify GST, parties, and HSN before accepting a draft.
Integrations
  • e-Way / e-Invoice credentials live in the company DB — use sandbox first.
  • SMTP and Google Drive credentials you enter are stored with company settings.
  • Phone-only Drive sync is last-writer-wins — not simultaneous multi-user.
App guardrails
  • Vouchers must balance before save; IRN/e-way can lock edits.
  • Follow-up messages are drafts — you send them.
  • No core telemetry. Network use is what you enable (AI, NIC, SMTP, Drive, Enterprise).
