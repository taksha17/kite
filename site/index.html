What’s free / what’s not

Plain talk for owners — not developer jargon. Full page: docs/whats-free.md.

Solo — free on one PC

Download, keep books on your computer, no yearly license just to open the file. You (or your CA) back up with Companies → Backup.

Limits (honest)

One desk, not a shared network file. No Kite phone helpline. Team needs Enterprise on a parent PC. AI / e-invoice need your keys when you turn them on.

Enterprise — you run it

Same open license; staff use browsers. You host the parent PC and its backups. Not a Kite-operated cloud.

Backup & restore steps: docs/backup-restore.md.

Caveats, guardrails & security

Clear limits — what Kite does and does not protect.

Installers & trust
  • Binaries are unsigned — expect SmartScreen / Gatekeeper warnings.
  • Download only from official GitHub Releases.
Data & backups
  • You own the SQLite files — back up deliberately (guide).
  • Do not put live DBs on a multi-writer network share for Solo.
  • Shared books → Enterprise, not file sharing.
  • Solo free limits → what’s free.
Enterprise / LAN
  • HTTP on a trusted LAN is convenient but not encrypted — use HTTPS beyond the office.
  • Passwords are PBKDF2-hashed; roles are not a wall against someone with the DB files.
AI (optional)
  • Off until you add a key. Draft-only — nothing auto-posts.
  • Prompts go to your provider when enabled.
